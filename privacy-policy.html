Privacy Policy

A plain-language account of what this app stores, where it lives, and who else ever sees it. Short answer: nobody but you — there's no server.

01Overview

There's no server, so there's nothing to collect

Buzzer Beater Fitness is a single-device workout tracker. It has no account system, no backend, and no company database — everything you enter lives in a private, on-device database that belongs to the app on your iPhone. We (the developer) never see it, because it never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it yourself.

This policy describes exactly what the app stores locally, the few things that ever cross your device's boundary, and the controls you have over all of it.

02What's stored

Only what you put in, to run the app you asked for

Everything below exists solely to power the app's own features — building plans, logging sets, and charting your progress. None of it is used for anything else.

CategoryExamples
ProfileName, height, fitness goal, unit preference (lb/kg)
PlansTraining splits, days, exercises, supersets, drop sets, per-muscle volume targets
Workout historySessions, sets, weights, reps, rest times, pre/post-workout feel
Body metricsWeigh-ins you log on the Progress tab
Cardio & mobilityDuration, distance, heart rate, and drill logs you enter
Custom exercisesAny exercise you add beyond the built-in catalog

The app does not read anything else on your phone. It has no access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera, and doesn't ask for any.

03Where it lives

On your phone, in the app's own sandbox

Data is stored using Apple's SwiftData framework, inside the storage area iOS gives exclusively to this app — no other app can read it, and it's protected by your device's own passcode/Face ID encryption at rest, the same as any other app's local data.

A note on device backups. If you have iCloud or Finder backup turned on for your iPhone, this app's local data is included in that backup like any other app's — that's between you and your own iCloud account. We have no access to your iCloud, and no copy of your data exists anywhere we control.

04Sign-in

Sign in with Apple or Google is a local label, not an account

If you choose Sign in with Apple or Google during setup, the app stores the identifier that provider hands back — purely so it can show your name and recognize "you" locally. There's no account server on our end to send it to, so that identifier is never verified, transmitted, or checked against anything; it sits in the same on-device database as everything else and never leaves your phone.

05Notifications & Live Activities

Scheduled on your phone, not routed through us

The rest-timer alert and the Lock Screen / Dynamic Island workout timer are scheduled directly on your device using Apple's local notification and Live Activity frameworks. No push-notification service is involved, and no workout data is sent anywhere to make them work.

06Sharing & export

Nothing goes out unless you tap share

Two features can send data off your device, and both require you to act first:

  • Plan Summary renders a shareable image of one plan, handed to iOS's own share sheet — you choose where it goes, if anywhere.
  • Export writes a full backup file you can save or send yourself, entirely under your control.

Neither happens automatically, and neither talks to a server of ours — there isn't one.

07What we don't do

No trackers, no selling data

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of any kind
  • No third-party integrations that receive your data
  • No selling, renting, or otherwise monetizing your information — there's nothing to sell, we never have it
08Your control

Delete it from the app, or just delete the app

Settings → Delete All Data wipes the on-device database after a multi-step confirmation. Deleting the app itself removes the database entirely, since the app's sandbox is the only place it ever existed.

09Children

Not directed at children

Buzzer Beater Fitness is intended for general audiences and isn't directed at children under 13. It doesn't knowingly collect information from children — in practice, it doesn't collect information from anyone, on- or off-device, as described above.

10Changes

If that ever changes, this page changes first

If a future version adds a feature that transmits data off your device — iCloud sync or a connected service, for example — this policy will be updated to describe it, and the version notes will call it out, before that feature ships.
